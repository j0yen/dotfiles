# LinkedIn Posting — the 2026 Brew 360 lens

## Core frame

LinkedIn in 2026 is a precision relevance engine, not a broadcast platform.
Brew 360 — LinkedIn's AI ranking system — reads semantic meaning, author
credibility, and audience fit, not just engagement counts. Organic reach on
company pages is down 60% since 2024; personal profiles now own 65% of feed
consumption and produce 8x the engagement. The strategy is: post 3-4x/week
from your personal profile, optimize for saves and substantive comments (not
likes), build around 3-5 content pillars, and treat the first 210 characters
("See more" fold) as the entire audition. Depth beats frequency — Brew 360
will resurface a good post for weeks if engagement keeps compounding.

## Key concepts

- **Brew 360** — LinkedIn's 2026 AI content-ranking system. Scores content
  semantics, author credibility, audience relevance.
- **Three-phase distribution** — (1) Quality Gate, (2) Golden Hour initial
  distribution to 5-15% of network, (3) Extended distribution to 2nd/3rd
  degree and topic feeds if engagement quality holds.
- **Golden Hour** — first 60 minutes after posting. Algorithm weighs initial
  engagement quality here. Reply to comments inside this window.
- **Engagement signal hierarchy** (most → least powerful):
  comment thread (multi-reply) > substantive comment (15x like) >
  Save (5x like) > Share-with-commentary > Send/DM-share > Dwell time >
  "See more" click > Like (baseline).
- **Dwell time** — how long users actually read. Long-read posts (2,500+
  char) get 42% more saves/shares.
- **See more fold (~210 chars)** — progressive disclosure cutoff. Everything
  before it must hook; everything after must reward.
- **Carousel / Document Post** — multi-page PDF rendered as swipeable slides.
  Highest-engagement format at 6.60%.
- **Content Pillars** — 3-5 repeatable topics that train both Brew 360 and
  your audience to associate you with a domain.
- **Contextual Keywords** — Brew 360 now reads industry terms in your prose
  for topic classification. Writing naturally with the right vocabulary
  matters more than hashtag stacking.
- **Pyramid Hashtag Strategy** — 1 broad + 2 mid-tier + 1-2 niche. 3-5 total.
  Past 5 triggers spam detection.
- **4:1:1 mix** — 4 value posts : 1 personal post : 1 promotional post. Cap
  promo at ~16%.
- **Creator Mode** — profile setting that unlocks Follow, newsletter, Live.
  Always on for thought leadership.

## Hook patterns (verbatim — preserve)

- **Contrarian take** — "The semantic layer is dead. Here's what's replacing it."
- **Specific number** — "We ran 500 queries against 3 LLMs. The results surprised us."
- **Admission of failure** — "I was wrong about dbt. Here's why."
- **Question** — "What happens when AI agents need data they can't trust?"
- **Bold claim** — "95% of enterprise AI projects fail for the same reason."
- **Pattern interrupt** — "Stop building agents. Build skills instead."
- **Outcome tease** — "One architecture decision saved our customer 60% on compute."

**Never open with:** "I'm excited to announce...", a hashtag, a tag-train of
10 people, or "In today's fast-paced world...".

## Post-structure patterns

- **AIDA (LinkedIn-adapted)** — Attention (hook, 2 lines) → Interest (why care,
  2-3 lines) → Detail (substance/framework/story, bulk) → Action (CTA, 1-2 lines).
- **1-3-1** — 1 hook line / 3 supporting points with line breaks / 1 CTA or
  question.
- **Story → Lesson** — specific situation → what you learned → how reader
  applies it.

## CTA menu (pick to match your goal)

- Opinion request — "What's your take? Am I wrong?" → comments
- Experience share — "Has anyone else seen this pattern?" → comments + dwell
- Save prompt — "Save this for your next data architecture review" → saves
- Tag prompt — "Tag someone who needs to hear this" → reach
- Binary choice — "Agree or disagree?" → comments
- Resource offer — "Drop a comment and I'll send you the full framework" →
  comments + DMs

## Format & cadence heuristics

- **Format mix:** 30-40% carousels, 30% text, 15-20% short video, 10-15%
  image, occasional article (1-2/mo).
- **Carousels:** 6-10 slides; 1080x1080 or 1080x1350; min 28px text; one
  idea per slide; hook on slide 1; CTA on final slide; export as PDF.
- **Video:** 15s-2min, captions mandatory (85% watched muted), vertical/
  square, hook in first 3s, native upload (no YouTube links).
- **Text:** 1,200-2,000 char standard; 2,500+ for deep thought leadership
  (+42% saves/shares); short paragraphs; line breaks between every paragraph;
  use →, •, ✓, ↳ for hierarchy (no bold/italic on LinkedIn); external links
  in first comment, never post body.
- **Cadence sweet spot:** 3-4x/week. Space ≥24h apart. 1x/wk is too low;
  daily fragments impressions.
- **Best days:** Wed (highest) > Thu > Fri afternoon. Avoid Mon and weekends.
- **Best times** (audience local): 10-11 AM peak; 3-5 PM strong second
  window; 7-8 AM and 12-1 PM solid; 6-8 PM growing in 2026.

## Algorithmic anti-triggers (Brew 360 suppresses)

- Engagement bait ("Like if you agree", "Comment YES for...")
- More than 5 hashtags
- External links in the post body (put them in first comment)
- More than 1 post per 24 hours
- Engagement pods / artificial reciprocal-like patterns
- Opening with a hashtag or a tag-train

## Personal profile vs company page

| Dimension | Personal | Company page |
|---|---|---|
| Engagement | 8x higher | baseline |
| Trust | 3x more trusted | lower |
| Algorithm favor | 65% of feed | 5% of feed |
| Recommendation | **primary channel** | amplification only |

Post from the personal profile. Have the company page repost selectively.

## Hashtag pyramid

| Layer | Count | Followers | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Broad | 1 | 1M+ | #DataEngineering |
| Mid-tier | 2 | 50K-500K | #SemanticLayer, #TextToSQL |
| Niche | 1-2 | 5K-50K | #DataMesh, #OntologyEngineering |

Place at end of post. Write naturally with industry terms in prose — that's
how Brew 360 actually classifies you now.

## Content pillars for an enterprise data/AI PM

- **AI + Semantic Layer** — text-to-SQL, MCP, agent architecture
- **Competitive intelligence** — Fabric IQ, dbt SL, Genie/Cortex "good enough"
- **PM craft** — PRDs, prioritization, strategy frameworks
- **Ontology / knowledge graphs** — BFO, OWL, KG+LLM (uniquely differentiable)
- **Industry commentary** — conference recaps, vendor moves, hot takes

## The 30-min daily engagement routine

- 10 min — substantive comments on 5-10 posts in your network ("Great post!"
  doesn't count)
- 5 min — reply to every comment on your own posts (inside Golden Hour
  ideally)
- 10 min — engage on content from people you don't follow (topic hashtag
  search)
- 5 min — 2-3 DMs (follow-ups, private shares)

Substantive comments do double duty: they boost the original (reciprocal
goodwill) and surface your profile to that post's audience.

## Success metrics

- Engagement rate > 5% (top quartile)
- Saves > 2% of impressions
- Comments > 10/post average
- Follower growth 2-5% monthly
- Inbound DMs — the ultimate metric

Timeline: months 1-2 establish cadence (low engagement, find voice); 3-4
compounding starts; 5-6 inbound DMs begin; 7-12 thought-leadership position
established.

## When to apply

- Drafting a single LinkedIn post (hook, structure, CTA)
- Critiquing an existing draft for fold-survival, format fit, CTA mismatch
- Choosing format (carousel vs text vs video) for a given topic
- Picking posting time/day for an audience
- Designing or rebalancing a posting program (cadence, pillars, mix)
- Repurposing long-form content (essay, PRD, deck) into LinkedIn posts
- Deciding personal-profile vs company-page distribution
- Diagnosing why a post underperformed (hook? CTA? signal mix? wrong format?)

## Out of scope

- Tweet/X thread drafting (different platform mechanics)
- Long-form blog or essay structure (use a writing-craft skill)
- Paid LinkedIn ad copy (different distribution model)
- Cold outbound DMs or sales-sequence outreach
- Generic personal-branding or career-coaching advice
